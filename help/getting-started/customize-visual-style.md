# Customize your concierge's visual style

Your Adobe Brand Concierge starts with visual choices Adobe already made for you — a color palette, fonts, and card images pulled from your website. In this video, you review those choices and customize the welcome message and starter prompt cards to better match your brand.

## Who is this video for?

* Brand and marketing teams
* Content designers and copywriters
* Anyone fine-tuning how their concierge looks

## Video content

* How Brand Concierge sources its starter colors, fonts, and card images from your website
* How to test the default desktop and mobile appearance
* How to edit the welcome message heading and subheading, including text color, weight, and size
* How to add, remove, and edit starter prompt cards — including card text, images, and background color
