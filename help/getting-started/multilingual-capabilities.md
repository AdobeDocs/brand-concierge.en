# Multilingual capabilities

Adobe Brand Concierge can respond in the language selected when you create a concierge. The knowledge source can be in another language, while responses are localized for visitors. This video demonstrates creating English and French concierges from the same English-language website and comparing their responses.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketers and content owners creating multilingual concierge experiences
* Teams evaluating localized customer conversations
* Anyone who wants to provide responses in a language different from the knowledge source

## Video content

* How to create English and French Brand Concierge instances using the same website as the knowledge source
* How the selected response language affects answers and starter prompt cards
* How imported knowledge sources, such as catalogs and website URLs, support localized responses
