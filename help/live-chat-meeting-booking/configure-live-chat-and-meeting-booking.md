# Configure live chat and meeting booking

Learn how to configure live chat and meeting booking in Adobe Brand Concierge and connect both integrations to the Handoff to Sales skill.

## Who is this video for?

* Users who configure concierge integrations and sales handoff skills

## Video content

* Check that representatives have connected their calendars and set live chat availability
* Configure meeting booking and live chat integrations and choose round robin, direct agent, or custom routing
* Add the integrations to the Handoff to Sales skill and define the intents that trigger it
* Review automatically generated instructions, refine which intents trigger each integration, and test the skill in the preview
