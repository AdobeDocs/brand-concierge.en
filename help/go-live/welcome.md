# Welcome to your go-live journey

Get ready to go live with Adobe Brand Concierge, an AI-powered assistant that lives on your website and answers your visitors' questions using your own content and brand voice. Going live is a short, guided process, and this video provides an overview of that journey before you start.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketing and digital experience leaders
* Project and program managers planning a launch
* Anyone new to the Brand Concierge go-live process

## Video content

* The four phases of going live: prepare, configure, validate, and roll out
* What you provide versus what your Adobe team configures
* The typical six-to-nine-week timeline and what affects it
