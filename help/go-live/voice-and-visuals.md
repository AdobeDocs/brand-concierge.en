# Shaping your voice and widget

Your concierge should not sound like a generic chatbot—it should sound like your brand. In this video, you shape two things: how your Adobe Brand Concierge speaks, and how its widget looks and behaves on your site.

## Who is this video for?

* Brand and marketing teams
* Content designers and copywriters
* Anyone defining the concierge's tone and appearance

## Video content

* How to describe your brand voice, including phrases to always and never use
* Widget visual choices: placement, display mode, color, and font
* Naming the assistant, writing its greeting, and choosing starter prompts
