# Measuring quality and setting guardrails

Before you launch Adobe Brand Concierge, you need a way to measure whether it gives good answers and rules that keep it safe, on-brand, and compliant. To measure answer quality, you build a golden set. To handle sensitive visitor questions, you define guardrails. This video covers both.

## Who is this video for?

* Brand and marketing teams
* Content owners and reviewers
* Legal and compliance stakeholders
* Anyone responsible for approving the concierge before launch

## Video content

* What a golden set is: real visitor questions paired with ideal answers
* How many question-and-answer pairs to include and which categories to cover
* Why out-of-scope examples matter and how the concierge declines them
* Using an AI-generated first draft of your golden set and refining it
