# Preparing your content and visitor journey

Adobe Brand Concierge can only answer questions as well as the content you provide it. Before it goes live, you prepare two things: the knowledge sources it draws from, and a short definition of the visitor experience you want to deliver.

## Who is this video for?

* Content and website owners
* Marketing teams preparing to launch a concierge
* Anyone gathering source material for Brand Concierge

## Video content

* The three types of knowledge sources: website URLs, PDFs, and product spreadsheets
* How to prepare your content before you share it
* How to write a one-page visitor journey definition
